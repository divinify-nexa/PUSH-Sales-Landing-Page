Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

PUSH Sales Collective LLC ("Push Sales," "we," "us," "our") is a Florida direct sales company based in Miami. We provide direct sales, customer acquisition, and marketing services for our business clients, and we recruit candidates for our own open positions. This policy explains what we collect from candidates, clients, and site visitors, how we use it, and your choices. It covers https://pushsales.co and the phone, email, and SMS communication our team sends. Use of this website is also governed by our Terms and Conditions.

1. Information We Collect

Information you give us: name, email address, phone number, application details (work history, availability, references), and the content of any message or form you submit.

Information collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, referring page, pages visited, and visit dates and times.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information. Please do not include it in a form.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Evaluate applications for open positions
  • Schedule and coordinate interviews, including confirmations and reminders
  • Contact you about your application or inquiry by phone, email, or SMS (SMS only with your consent)
  • Respond to questions and inquiries
  • Operate, secure, and improve this website
  • Comply with legal obligations

A person reviews every application. We do not use automated decision-making.

3. SMS Communications

Push Sales offers an optional SMS program for candidates. If you enroll, we may send text messages of these types only:

  • Interview scheduling, confirmations, and reminders
  • Updates on the status of your application
  • Replies to questions or inquiries you send us

How you opt in

Only by checking the clearly labeled SMS consent checkbox on our website contact form. The box is never pre-checked. We never enroll anyone from a resume, referral, purchased list, or any other source.

No mobile information, phone numbers, or SMS opt-in/consent data will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This data will not be sold, rented, or disclosed to any third party for their own marketing use under any circumstances. All other categories of sharing described in this policy exclude SMS opt-in data and consent.

Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of employment, of submitting an application, or of being interviewed.

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact hiring@pushsales.co or (305) 850-7699.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message. You will receive one confirmation message and no further texts. Opting out of SMS does not withdraw your application; we may still reach you by phone or email.

4. Email Communications

You can unsubscribe from promotional email at any time using the unsubscribe link or by emailing hiring@pushsales.co. Transactional messages about an active application (interview details, status updates, replies) may still be sent.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that host this site, deliver our email and SMS, or store applicant records, solely to perform services for us (never for their own marketing, and never including SMS opt-in data for marketing purposes)
  • When required by law, subpoena, or court order, or to protect our legal rights
  • With internal staff who need it to evaluate your application or inquiry
  • In a merger, acquisition, or reorganization, where the successor remains bound by this policy

6. Data Retention & Security

We keep information only as long as needed to evaluate your candidacy or inquiry and to meet legal recordkeeping obligations. We use access controls, encryption in transit, and security-vetted vendors. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies to operate the site and understand page usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

8. Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete your information, or opt out of SMS, email, or all contact. Email hiring@pushsales.co with your request and the name and email you used. We will respond within 45 days. Exercising a right never affects how we evaluate your application.

9. Age Restrictions

This website and our application process are for people 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 and will delete it if we learn we have.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date above. Material changes to our SMS program will be communicated to enrolled recipients before they take effect.
